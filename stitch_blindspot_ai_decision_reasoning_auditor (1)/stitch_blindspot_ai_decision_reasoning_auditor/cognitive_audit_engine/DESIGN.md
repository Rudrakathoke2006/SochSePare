## Brand & Style

This design system embodies intellectual rigor, analytical clarity, and systematic transparency. Designed for high-stakes decision makers, strategists, and executive evaluators, the interface functions as a cognitive mirror rather than an advisory oracle: it does not render verdicts or make choices; it deconstructs reasoning architectures, highlights unstated dependencies, and maps internal cognitive tensions.

The aesthetic philosophy merges **Modern Precision Minimalism** with **Technical/Analytical Brutalism**:
- Razor-sharp structural grid lines, disciplined information density, and deliberate typography evoke the gravity of legal discovery briefs, institutional research platforms, and cryptographic ledgers.
- Warm ivory and stark white foreground elements float over deep charcoal and tectonic slate backdrops, ensuring prolonged visual focus without cognitive fatigue.
- Diagnostic semantic indicators act as surgical cues across cognitive categories: Warning Amber (Assumptions), Terracotta (Overlooked Factors), Violet (Internal Conflicts), Slate-Blue (Missing Information), and Emerald (Verified Facts/Coverage).
- Tactile qualities prioritize restraint: fine-gauge borders, crisp monospaced telemetry data, hairline dividing rules, and zero frivolous ornamentation.

## Colors

The palette leverages a dark-first environment that emphasizes categorical analysis through dedicated diagnostic hues.

### Foundation & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base (`#12151A`)**: Deep tectonic obsidian slate providing an unyielding, non-reflective baseline.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#181D24`)**: Base container fill for audit panels, side rails, and decision matrices.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#202630`)**: Elevated cards, interactive segments, and modal tiers.
- **Surface Elevation 3 (`#2B3340`)**: Hover states, active node surfaces, and pill toggles.
- **Border Subdued (`#28303C`)**: Hairline boundary lines defining structural regions.
- **Border Crisp (`#3D4756`)**: Active element borders, divider rules, and analytical tree branches.

### Typography & Content
- **Text Primary (`#F4F3EF`)**: Warm ivory for primary headlines, core arguments, and critical logic statements.
- **Text Secondary (`#9EAAB7`)**: Neutral silver-slate for descriptive prose, metadata labels, and summaries.
- **Text Muted (`#626F7E`)**: Deep slate for disabled states, secondary telemetry, and tree connective keys.

### Diagnostic Semantic Accent Matrix
- **Known Facts / Coverage (Emerald - `#10B981`)**: Grounded reality, verified assertions, documented data.
- **Assumptions (Warning Amber - `#F59E0B`)**: Unproven premises, extrapolated inputs, risk hypotheses.
- **Overlooked Factors (Terracotta - `#D97736`)**: Neglected externalities, blind spots, second-order consequences.
- **Internal Conflicts (Violet - `#A855F7`)**: Paradoxical requirements, mutually exclusive goals, logical inconsistencies.
- **Missing Information (Slate-Blue - `#60A5FA`)**: Informational voids, unasked questions, data gaps.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances structural authority with mathematical scrutiny:

- **Headlines (`Manrope`)**: Provides geometric precision, tight apertures, and calm authority. Used for reasoning node headers, macro section dividers, and canvas titles.
- **Body & Long-form Analysis (`Geist`)**: Delivers supreme legibility across dense paragraphs, premise breakdowns, and cross-examination clauses.
- **Analytical Accents & Badges (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Embeds empirical rigor into telemetry indicators, system metadata, confidence scores, and logic condition tokens.

All headings must apply negative tracking to reinforce compactness. Monospace tokens must strictly maintain uppercase styling when used as classification markers or taxonomy tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model adheres to a rigorous 12-column modular grid designed for asymmetrical, data-rich exploration (e.g., a permanent 3-column inquiry panel paired with an 8-column decision-tree visualizer, anchored by a 1-column global telemetry spine).

- **Desktop (1280px+)**: 12-column fluid grid, `margin: 2.5rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Enables dual-pane analytical review (input transcript vs. decomposed logic graph).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8-column adaptive layout, `margin: 1.5rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Logic trees collapse into vertically scrollable branch cards.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column responsive stream, `margin: 1rem`, `gutter: 0.75rem`. Complex tree maps convert into sequential step-down accordions with pill-navigation anchors.

Internal rhythm uses a rigid 4px base increment. Spacing tokens (`space-xs` through `space-xl`) govern component encapsulation, ensuring cards and panels feel deliberate, compact, and engineered.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects exaggerated drop shadows and artificial skeuomorphism in favor of **Tonal Layering** and **Crisp Low-Contrast Outlines**:

- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base)**: `#12151A` with a micro-dot matrix pattern (`#1C222B`, 1px dots spaced every 24px) providing architectural grounding.
- **Layer 1 (Modular Paneling)**: `#181D24` enclosed by a 1px solid border of `#28303C`.
- **Layer 2 (Audit Cards & Floating Menus)**: `#202630` framed with `#3D4756`. Subtle directional rim lighting is achieved via an interior inset box shadow: `inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(244, 243, 239, 0.05)`.
- **Active / Dragged Nodes**: A razor-sharp 1px border highlighted with the relevant semantic diagnostic color, paired with a targeted ambient glow: `0 0 16px -2px [diagnostic-color-with-20%-alpha]`.
- **Decision Map Connector Lines**: Orthogonal 1.5px lines rendered in `#2E3744`, shifting to semantic accent hues upon branch inspection or cross-node conflict tracing.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a low-radius, architectural profile (`roundedness: 1`):

- **Default Form Factor (`0.25rem` / 4px)**: Buttons, text fields, inline badges, diagnostic indicators, and tree nodes.
- **Containers & Audit Panels (`0.5rem` / 8px)**: Master cards, modal windows, reasoning tree group containers.
- **Large Overlays (`0.75rem` / 12px)**: Macro audit sheets and canvas dialogs.
- **Strictly No Pill Shapes**: Buttons, chips, and pills never exceed 4px border radius. Rounded profiles are kept crisp and square-biased to preserve structural sobriety.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Background `#F4F3EF`, text `#12151A`, font `Geist` Medium 14px, radius 4px. Focus state exhibits an external 2px offset border in `#60A5FA`.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Background `transparent`, border 1px solid `#3D4756`, text `#F4F3EF`. On hover: surface shifts to `#202630` with border `#738A9C`.
- **Diagnostic Action Trigger**: Bound to the respective semantic hue (e.g., violet border for "Flag Internal Conflict"), retaining a translucent fill (`rgba([hex], 0.08)`).

### Analytical Chips & Badges
- Constructed with `JetBrains Mono` at 11px uppercase with `0.06em` letter spacing.
- Bound to the 5 diagnostic pillars:
  - **Assumption**: Border `#F59E0B`, text `#FBBF24`, background `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.1)`.
  - **Overlooked Factor**: Border `#D97736`, text `#FB923C`, background `rgba(217, 119, 54, 0.1)`.
  - **Internal Conflict**: Border `#A855F7`, text `#C084FC`, background `rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.1)`.
  - **Missing Info**: Border `#60A5FA`, text `#93C5FD`, background `rgba(96, 165, 250, 0.1)`.
  - **Verified Fact**: Border `#10B981`, text `#34D399`, background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.1)`.
- Sizing: 20px height, 6px horizontal padding, 2px border radius.

### Structured Audit Cards
- Surface `#181D24`, 1px perimeter border `#28303C`.
- Left edge features an integrated 3px semantic strip matching the assigned category.
- Header contains an empirical classification label (e.g., `CONFLICT // #04B`) in `JetBrains Mono`, an unvarnished claim title in `Manrope` 16px, and collapsible logic premises in `Geist` 14px.

### Inputs & Prompt Fields
- Multi-line analysis terminal: background `#12151A`, border 1px solid `#28303C`, text `#F4F3EF`, placeholder `#626F7E`.
- Active focus state: border shifts to `#738A9C`, accompanied by an indicator displaying input token mass and logic statement counts in `JetBrains Mono`.

### Decision-Map Tree Nodes
- Orthogonally linked rectangular cards (`#202630`) measuring a strict width of 280px.
- Connectors: SVG paths with square joints, 1.5px stroke `#2E3744`.
- Tensions between conflicting nodes are visualized by a dashed dual-tone Violet-Terracotta connector line that pulses on mouse inspection.

### Interactive Navigation Tabs
- Flat horizontal rail with no pill container; underline indicator style.
- Inactive tabs: text `#9EAAB7` in `Manrope` 14px.
- Active tab: text `#F4F3EF` with a 2px high accent underline in `#F4F3EF` and an inline numeric counter in `JetBrains Mono`.
